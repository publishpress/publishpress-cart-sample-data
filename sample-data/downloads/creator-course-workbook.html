Creator Course Workbook

Turn an idea into a course people can complete and recommend.

1. Define the outcome

What can a learner do after finishing your course?

2. Map the lessons

List three milestones between the starting point and the outcome.

3. Design one exercise

Choose a small action that lets learners practice the first milestone.

4. Plan the launch

Write your launch date, first audience, and one way you will collect feedback.
